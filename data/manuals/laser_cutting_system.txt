MACHINE: Laser Cutting System
MODEL: LC-500

ERROR CODE: L110
SECTION: Fehlercode L110 - Kollision des Schneidkopfs
PAGE: 1
MEANING: L110 is triggered when the collision sensor on the cutting head detects contact with the workpiece or the workpiece support outside of the programmed cutting path. The control system immediately stops all axis movements and raises the cutting head to the safe position.
CAUSES:
- Warped or unevenly placed sheet metal on the workpiece support.
- Incorrect material thickness specification in the cutting program.
- Loose or damaged collision sensor mount.

SECTION: Fehlercode L110 - Kollision des Schneidkopfs
PAGE: 1
STEPS:
1. Press the emergency stop and confirm the de-energized state according to the safety instructions.
2. Check the workpiece for warping or improper placement.
3. Compare the material thickness stored in the cutting program with the actual sheet thickness.
4. Check the collision sensor for a secure fit and damage.
5. Reset the error via the CU-500 user interface (Diagnostics > Clear Error) and move the cutting head manually over the workpiece surface to confirm free movement.

ERROR CODE: L145
SECTION: Fehlercode L145 - Übertemperatur des Laserresonators
PAGE: 1
MEANING: L145 is triggered when the temperature of the laser resonator exceeds 55°C. The control system automatically reduces the laser power starting at 48°C as an early warning before the forced shutdown occurs at 55°C.
CAUSES:
- Insufficient coolant flow due to clogged filters.
- Coolant temperature of the external chiller is too high.
- Continuous operation above the permissible duty cycle for the set laser power.

SECTION: Fehlercode L145 - Übertemperatur des Laserresonators
PAGE: 1
STEPS:
1. Switch off the system and allow the resonator to cool down for at least 20 minutes.
2. Check the coolant filters and clean or replace them if necessary.
3. Check the target temperature of the chiller according to the manufacturer's specifications.
4. If the error occurs repeatedly, check the cutting program for excessive duty cycle at high power and adjust if necessary.

ERROR CODE: L210
SECTION: Fehlercode L210 - Kommunikationsringfehler
PAGE: 1
MEANING: L210 indicates that the EtherCAT ring topology check has failed — the control system cannot confirm a closed ring between its IN and OUT ports. This typically occurs immediately after switching on or after cabling work on the line.
CAUSES:
- A disconnected or damaged EtherCAT cable in the ring.
- A downstream device in the ring is switched off or is in an error state.
- Swapped IN/OUT port cabling during installation.

SECTION: Fehlercode L210 - Kommunikationsringfehler
PAGE: 1
STEPS:
1. Check whether all devices in the EtherCAT ring are switched on.
2. Check the cabling at each ring transition, starting at the OUT port of the CU-500 towards the downstream devices.
3. Compare the IN/OUT port cabling with the line topology plan (Appendix A).
4. If the error is cleared by disconnecting a single downstream device, its control system and cabling must be checked separately.
