SECTION: E301 Overview
MACHINE: RobotArm-300
MODEL: R700
ERROR CODE: E301
MEANING: Axis 1 joint limit exceeded.
CAUSES:
- Program trajectory out of working envelope
- Joint encoder calibration drift

SECTION: E301 Troubleshooting
MACHINE: RobotArm-300
MODEL: R700
ERROR CODE: E301
STEPS:
1. Switch controller to manual teach pendant mode.
2. Jog Axis 1 back within soft limit range (-170 deg to +170 deg).
3. Reset motion controller fault state.

SECTION: E302 Overview
MACHINE: RobotArm-300
MODEL: R700
ERROR CODE: E302
MEANING: End-effector payload sensor error.
CAUSES:
- Payload weight exceeds maximum rated 10kg
- Strain gauge sensor cable disconnected

SECTION: E302 Troubleshooting
MACHINE: RobotArm-300
MODEL: R700
ERROR CODE: E302
STEPS:
1. Verify payload mass does not exceed rated limits.
2. Inspect flange cable connector for loose pins.
3. Perform load sensor tare calibration.
